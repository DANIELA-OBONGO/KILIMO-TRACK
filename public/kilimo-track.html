Smart Farming Starts with Good Data

KilimoTrack helps you record planting seasons, monitor soil acidity, and log pest outbreaks — all in one modern dashboard. Track every aspect of your farm and grow smarter.

Everything a Kenyan Farmer Needs

From long rains to short rains — track every season, every field, every threat.

🌱 Season & Planting Tracker

Record crop types, planting dates, expected harvest dates, and season types — Long Rains, Short Rains, Dry Season, or Irrigation.

🧪 Soil Acidity Records

Log pH levels per field and get automatic recommendations. Know exactly when to apply lime or sulfur for optimal crop growth.

🐛 Pest Outbreak Log

Track Fall Armyworm, Aphids, and other pest occurrences. Record severity, treatment applied, and mark outbreaks as resolved.

📊 Reports & Analytics

Get a bird's-eye view of your farm data — most common pests, harvest success rates, regional activity, and critical alerts.

📍 Regions Overview

View farming data by region — Rift Valley, Central Kenya, Meru, and more. Identify high-risk areas at a glance.

💾 MySQL Database Backed

All your data is securely stored in a MySQL database. Nothing is lost — every record, every season, every test is saved permanently.

How KilimoTrack Works

Simple steps to start tracking your farm like a pro.

1️⃣

Set Up Your Farm

Install on localhost using XAMPP. Import the database and you're ready in minutes.

2️⃣

Record Your Data

Add your planting seasons, soil pH tests, and any pest outbreaks as they happen.

3️⃣

Track & Improve

Use the Reports and Regions pages to spot patterns and make smarter farming decisions.

4️⃣

Stay Alert

Active pest alerts keep you informed. Resolve outbreaks and keep your records clean.
